A roguelike about being a tax auditor in hell. It is the best game of the year that I can barely recommend to anyone.

Every run is an audit: you cross-check demon receipts, find the tax dodge and collect in souls. The systems are absurdly deep and the humor lands. But it is an Epic with an asterisk: it takes over a hundred hours to show you everything, and full price is steep for a niche this small.
